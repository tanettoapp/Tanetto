Privacy policy

In short: your companion, goals and journal are saved on this phone; a copy ends up in a backup only if Android backup is on or if you create an account. Typing your name is optional, the chat sends nothing to anyone, and the only ads are short videos that you choose to watch (if you're under 13 there are none; if you don't say your age, they're never personalised). If something here isn't clear, ask an adult you trust. Tanetto is designed for ages 13 and up: for anyone younger, or anyone who doesn't say their age, it works in protected mode.

Data controller: Snugbloom Studio – Riccardo Giuseppe Signorello, Italy. Contact: tanettoapp@gmail.com.

What data: Tanetto also works without an account. In that case your companion, goals, journal, garden and progress are saved only in this device's memory: Tanetto doesn't send them to anyone. Thoughts written in “Let it go” are not saved at all. If Android backup is turned on on your phone, an encrypted copy of this data (including your age) is also kept in your Google account's backup, as for every app: Google manages it and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

If you type your name for the greeting (it's optional), it's stored the same way: on your device and, if you have an account, in the online backup.

Copy of your data: if you tap “Save a copy”, Tanetto creates a file with your progress directly on your device. The file stays with you and is not sent to anyone. With “Restore a copy”, the file is read only by your device.

Optional account and online backup: if you choose to create an account (with Google or with email and password), we keep your email address and a full copy of your app data, journal included (but not your age), only to let you sign in and restore it. The data is held by Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd.) on servers in the European Union: it travels encrypted and Google stores it encrypted. In the app only your account can read it, but it isn't encrypted with a key that only you have: technically, we (who run the service) and Google (as the provider) can also access it. Sign-in itself (Firebase Authentication) is handled by Google in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Legal basis: providing the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The backup stays until you delete your profile: you can do so anytime in Profile › Account and data › Manage account › Delete profile, or on the page tanetto.app/elimina-account.html. The account and the online backup are deleted immediately. If you delete your profile in the app, your progress on the phone is deleted too; if you use the web page instead, what's on the phone stays until you delete it yourself (see “Retention”).

Purchases: payments for Tanetto Plus and Tanì packs are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details: Google only tells the app which purchases are active, so it can unlock them.

Invite a friend (only for people aged 18 or over): the app reads once from Google Play, on the phone, the invite code it was downloaded with, and only uses it if you're 18 or over. If you use an invite, we store on Google Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC) the random invite code, a code derived from the phone's identifier (a pseudonym: it doesn't contain your name or any other data about you) and the dates. They are used only to give each reward once and to prevent abuse (for example the same phone inviting itself). No names, no contacts, no address book. This data deletes itself after 12 months.

Anonymous statistics: to understand whether the app is useful, we count some actions in aggregate form, for example how often the app is opened, whether it's used on the following days or which parts are used (such as the shop or the games), and we receive anonymous error reports (the type of error with a short technical description, the app version, whether it's the app or the website, and the line of code; never what you write). We use GoatCounter, a statistics service with no cookies and no profiles, run from Ireland with data on servers in the European Union (Finland and Germany). To send the count, the device connects to the service, which technically receives the IP address without storing it in an identifiable way. Legal basis: legitimate interest in improving the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR / UK GDPR). You can turn the statistics off at any time in Profile › Privacy and age. They are always off for anyone under the age of digital consent or who hasn't given their age, and no counts are sent before you answer the age question: they wait on the phone and are sent only if the statistics then turn out to be on; otherwise they're deleted.

Hosting: the app's pages are hosted by an external provider, which may log the IP address for technical and security reasons under its own privacy policy.

Cookies: Tanetto itself uses no tracking cookies. Local storage is used only to make the app work (and to avoid counting statistics twice) and is strictly necessary. In the Android app you can choose to watch short ads to get a small reward: they never appear on their own, only when you choose to watch one.

Rewarded videos (Android app only): ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC; data may also be processed in the United States). Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); transfers to the US rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Tanetto itself never receives your advertising ID. Where the law requires it (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland), we ask for your consent with Google's consent management tool (UMP) before any ad is shown; you can change it anytime in Profile › Privacy and age › Ad choices. To load and play the videos, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, your IP address and information about your interaction with the ads, to serve and measure them and prevent fraud and, only if you're 18 or over (and with your consent, where the law requires it), to personalise them. Legal basis: your consent, where required (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR / UK GDPR), and legitimate interest in offering optional rewards and preventing fraud (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / UK GDPR). You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your Android settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can watch up to 5 videos a day, and with Tanetto Plus you see no ads at all. Google may also collect diagnostic information about the ad service (for example crashes and performance) and estimate an approximate location from your IP address, for the same purposes. Under 13 there are no rewarded videos; if you'd rather not say your age, they are never personalised.

Retention: data on the device stays until you delete it. You can do so at any time with “Start over” (your age answer stays, though) or by clearing the app's data in your Android settings or your browser data. Copies in Android backup are managed by Google under its own rules. Statistics are only aggregate numbers.

Your rights: you can request information, access, correction, deletion and restriction, and object to processing, by writing to the contact above. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). You can also ask for a copy of your data in a portable format, and you can withdraw your consent at any time; this does not affect what was done before.

US state privacy rights: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, note that we do not sell your personal information for money. Showing reward videos through Google AdMob may count as “sharing” for targeted advertising under those laws. You can opt out by deleting or resetting your advertising ID in your Android settings, or in Profile › Privacy and age › Ad choices when the app shows it, and you can use the rights described above by writing to us. Using these rights will never change how we treat you.

Age and children: Tanetto is designed for people aged 13 and up and is not directed to children under 13. The first time, we ask for your month and year of birth, which are never sent to us: they stay on your device (and in Android backup, if it's on); you can also answer “I'd rather not say”. If you enter an age under 13, or you'd rather not say, Tanetto works in protected mode: no statistics, no account or online backup, no sharing outside the app (no images, no invitations) and no offers or nudges to buy (not even the free trial). Under 13 there are also no ads and no rewarded videos (the app requests no ads from Google AdMob and doesn't use the advertising ID). If you'd rather not say, though, rewarded videos stay available (you only watch them if you want) and are never personalised. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13: if you enter an age under 13, the app sends no information about you or what you do in the app, to us or to anyone else, apart from payments, which go through Google Play, and Android backup, if it's on, which Google manages. Under the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16, depending on where you live), ads are never personalised, statistics are off and the online backup isn't available. The chat with your companion only has pre-written replies and sends nothing to anyone. Tanì are game coins, not real money; Tanetto Plus and Tanì packs are paid only through Google Play. If you're a parent or guardian and think a child under 13 in your care has given us personal data, write to tanettoapp@gmail.com and we'll delete it right away. You can also write to us with any other question or request.

Changes: if this policy changes, the updated version will always be on this page. Last updated: 23/11/2026.
